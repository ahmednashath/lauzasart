About the Artist

Painting the living rhythm of the islands

Lauza is a Maldivian artist whose practice is rooted in observation, memory, and the tactile materiality of water and pigment. Her work moves between marine illustration, botanical studies, and expressive fine art painting, reflecting the careful choreography of reef life and island ecosystems.

Each composition begins with close looking: the curve of a coral branch, the shimmer of a tide, the soft geometry of leaves warmed by the sun. In studio practice, Lauza layers gouache, watercolor, and digital finishing processes to capture both intimacy and atmosphere, allowing sea and land to blur gently into a story of belonging.
